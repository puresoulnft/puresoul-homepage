Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pure Soul NFT? +

A collectible NFT project divided into three divine factions. Each faction contains its own characters and rarity structure.

How do I mint a Divine Ascendant? +

Click "Mint Now," connect your wallet, and submit 0.035 + GAS(.0187) to mint a randomized NFT.

Can I choose the NFT I mint? +

No — minting is randomized to ensure rarity fairness and uniqueness.

Why are there 3,333 Divine Ascendant NFTs if there aren't that many characters? +

Because Pure Soul works like a trading card game. Each NFT you mint has a unique ID, but characters can appear multiple times. There are only 50 unique Divine Ascendants characters in this drop, spread across the full supply of 3,333 NFTs. Think of it like opening card packs — you may pull the same character as someone else, but your NFT token is one-of-a-kind.

Why does my wallet say “This transaction reverted during simulation. Funds may be lost if submitted"? +

This message appears in some Solana wallets (like Phantom) during minting. It’s caused by how the wallet tries to “simulate” what a transaction will do before you approve it. Our minting process uses: • 🎲 Randomized NFT assignment • 🔀 Dynamic on-chain account creation • 🔐 Custom payment routing to our treasury wallet. These are advanced Solana features that wallet simulations don’t fully understand yet. As a result, the simulation often fails or can’t show you the correct preview — even though the transaction is totally valid. Rest assured: • ✅ Your NFT will still mint successfully • ✅ Your SOL will be sent to the correct treasury wallet • ✅ You will see your NFT appear in your wallet once the transaction is confirmed. This is not a bug or failure. It’s just a limitation of wallet simulations, and it happens with many advanced Solana mints — especially when randomness or new account creation is involved. We’re working within Solana’s standards, and everything is safe under the hood. 💡 Tip: If you’re unsure, you can always check the transaction on Solana Explorer using the signature after confirming it.

Are rarities random when I mint? +

No — rarities are pre-assigned based on dynamic percentage ranges:

  • %10 = Legendary
  • %25 = Epic
  • %40 = Rare
  • %25 = Common

When you mint, you get a random token ID, and that token ID determines your rarity and which character is pulled from that rarity pool.
